1 Introduction & Project Context

This report presents the pre-intervention baseline environmental assessment for the NBS4MED pilot site in {{ ctx.site }}, one of five sites across the Mediterranean basin (Spain, Türkiye, Greece, Tunisia, Egypt). All indicators are derived from satellite remote sensing (Landsat 8/9, MODIS, CHIRPS) and NASA POWER MERRA-2 climate reanalysis over a 30-year reference period. These results feed into the Self-Evaluation Tool (Activity 3.3.2) to size, justify, and monitor Nature-Based Solution interventions under the Interreg NEXT MED Programme.
